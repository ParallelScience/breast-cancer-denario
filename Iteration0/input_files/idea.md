**Title**: Deconvoluting Core Morphological Drivers of Malignancy in Breast FNA via Regularized Feature Group Analysis
**Description**: This research aims to precisely identify the minimal, representative `worst` nuclear morphology features that are most discriminative for breast cancer diagnosis. Leveraging inherent multicollinearity among features like `radius`/`perimeter`/`area` and `compactness`/`concavity`/`concave points`, we will employ group-aware regularization or hierarchical selection techniques within a rigorous nested cross-validation framework. The objective is to characterize the distinct diagnostic signatures manifested by these interdependent upper-tail morphological descriptors, moving beyond general feature importance to pinpoint core morphological patterns driving malignancy. Evaluation will prioritize robust sensitivity at high specificity and probability calibration, providing confidence intervals for these critical clinical metrics.
