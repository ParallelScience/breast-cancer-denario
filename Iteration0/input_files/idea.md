**Title**: Calibrated Abstention for Breast Cancer: Disentangling Tail-Feature Drivers of Diagnostic Confidence
**Description**:
This research proposes a calibrated selective prediction framework for breast cancer diagnosis from FNA images, specifically optimized for the clinically critical high-specificity operating regime and the severe cost asymmetry of false negatives. The model will quantify its predictive uncertainty, automatically deferring diagnostically ambiguous cases to human pathologists to maximize malignant recall while minimizing unnecessary biopsies. Critically, the study will unravel the individual and synergistic contributions of the 'worst' nuclear morphology features (e.g., `worst radius`, `worst concavity`, `worst texture`) that drive model confidence and deferral decisions, disentangling their true discriminative power despite inherent multicollinearity. Robust evaluation will utilize nested cross-validation to provide confidence intervals for sensitivity at high specificity, calibration, and deferral rates across varying cost thresholds.
